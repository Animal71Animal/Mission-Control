# WLP TikTok Strategy: 14-Day Warmup & Growth

## Target Accounts
1. **Kade Rivers** (AI Rock Artist)
2. **Aria Vale** (AI EDM/Electronic Artist)
3. **Madison Blair** (AI Pop Artist)
4. **ANIMAL** (DJ/Producer/Behind-the-Scenes)

## Phase 1: 7-Day Warmup (Account Calibration)
*Goal: Tell the algorithm who we are without posting content yet.*

- **Daily Behavior:** Spend 15-30 mins scrolling the FYP for each specific genre.
- **Engagement:** Like and comment on top creators in the niche (e.g., Kade engaged with rock/metal tiktok; Aria with synth/rave culture).
- **Profile Optimization:** Bio + link (once eligible) + high-quality avatar.

## Phase 2: Content Pillars (Days 8-14)

### 1. Kade Rivers (The Gritty Rocker)
- **Visuals:** Dark, industrial, high-contrast AI video clips.
- **Hook:** "How a virtual rocker sees the world."
- **Content:** Lyric snippets over raw footage, "tuning" sessions, genre mashups.

### 2. Aria Vale (The Ethereal EDM Architect)
- **Visuals:** Neon, futuristic, glitch-core aesthetic.
- **Hook:** "Building the future of the underground."
- **Content:** Sound design breakdowns, visualizer clips, "POV you're at a 2030 rave."

### 3. Madison Blair (The Modern Pop Star)
- **Visuals:** Clean, vibrant, "day-in-the-life" aesthetic (AI generated).
- **Hook:** "Can a ghost top the charts?"
- **Content:** "Relatable" trending audio covers, fashion/aesthetic montages, snippet reveals.

### 4. ANIMAL (The Architect)
- **Visuals:** Studio shots, Ableton screen recordings, SRB booth POV.
- **Hook:** "The human behind the machine."
- **Content:** Producing Kade/Aria/Madison, DJ gear tips, Wicked Liquid mission statements.
