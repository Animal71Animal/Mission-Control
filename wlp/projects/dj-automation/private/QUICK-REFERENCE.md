## The 9 Pain Points We Solve

| # | Pain Point | Our Solution |
|---|------------|--------------|
| A | Doesn't adapt to room | Energy slider (1–10), scenario modes, spending bumps |
| B | Dead air / bad transitions | Stage-safe tagging, set templates, hard guarantees |
| C | Clunky announcements | Musical-aware scheduler, dynamic frequency, multi-voice TTS |
| D | Rotation chaos | Built-in rotation board, per-dancer prefs, fairness logs |
| E | Music library pain | Policy profiles, anti-repeat engine, local taste learning |
| F | Reliability issues | True offline mode, failover audio, no updates during hours |
| G | Staff permissions | Role-based access (Manager, House Mom, Dancer, Bartender) |
| H | Integration gaps | POS/VIP hooks, multi-zone audio, lighting integration |
| I | Generic reporting | Shift recap, rotation metrics, operational alerts |
