# Artist Mood Boards & Content Launch

## 🎸 Kade Rivers (Rock / Industrial)
**Visual Vibe:** "Abandoned Boiler Room"
- **Colors:** Coal black, rusted copper, flickering cold blue.
- **Textures:** Raw concrete, scratched metal, distorted static, rain on glass.
- **Lighting:** Heavy shadows, high-contrast silhouettes, strobe effects.
- **Key Imagery:** Wires, heavy leather, grain silos, dark urban alleys.

### Script 1: The Intro
*   **Visual:** Silhouette of Kade in a dark, humid space. Grainy film filter applied.
*   **Hook (Text on Screen):** "They say ghosts can't play rock..."
*   **Audio:** Heavy, distorted bass riff from his debut single.
*   **Action:** Kade leans into a mic that isn't plugged in. Everything glitches to black.
*   **Caption:** Just getting started. #KadeRivers #AIArtist #RockRevival

## 🌌 Aria Vale (EDM / Electronic)
**Visual Vibe:** "The Underground Continuum"
- **Colors:** Deep obsidian, industrial grey, flashes of strobe white, muted teal.
- **Textures:** Raw concrete, sweat-fogged glass, vibrating speaker mesh, analog cable tangle.
- **Lighting:** Drifting warehouse smoke, singular powerful strobes, low-ceiling shadows (not neon-focused).
- **Key Imagery:** Authentic warehouse stacks, close-ups of analog gear buttons, silhouettes in heavy fog, "raw" rave culture energy.

### Script 1: The Sound Drop
*   **Visual:** Tight, vibrating shot of an analog synth knob turning. The background is a dark, raw warehouse space with drifting smoke.
*   **Hook (Text on Screen):** "Pure frequency. No filters."
*   **Audio:** A clean, soaring synth lead that suddenly drops into a heavy, resonant tech-house beat.
*   **Action:** As the beat drops, the frame shakes with the bass. Aria is visible only as a silhouette moving in the smoke behind the gear.
*   **Caption:** Found in the warehouse. 🌌 #AriaVale #TechHouse #UndergroundRave #WLP
