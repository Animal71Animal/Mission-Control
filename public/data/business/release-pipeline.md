Sync Licensing PipelineSync is one of the highest-value revenue channels for independent artists. A single TV/film placement can outperform months of streaming revenue.🎬 Step 1: Track AssessmentBefore pitching any track for sync, evaluate:Is it 100% cleared? No uncleared samples. Period.Do you control both master AND publishing? (or do you have written permission from all rights holders?)Does it have lyrics? If yes, are they free of brand names, profanity (for certain markets), or dated references?Instrumental version available? Always deliver a clean instrumental.Alternate edits available? 30-sec, 60-sec, 2-min versions are standard requests.🎬 Step 2: Metadata for SyncSync supervisors search by keywords.
