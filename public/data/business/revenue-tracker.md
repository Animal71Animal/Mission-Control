## 1. Revenue Categories

### 1.1 Performance Income

| Sub-Category | Description | Notes |
|--------------|-------------|-------|
| Club / Bar Sets | One-off DJ bookings at clubs, bars, lounges | Mynt Lounge, etc. |

| Corporate Events | Brand activations, trade shows, galas | B2B contracts |
