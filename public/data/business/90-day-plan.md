This plan covers all three streams. Music operations (streams 1 & 2) are in Phases 1-3 below. Software milestones run in a parallel track.

## Risk Flags & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Artist Identity Confusion** | Keep each artist's visual/sonic identity sharp and distinct. Don't cross-contaminate aesthetics. Each artist should feel like a real, separate entity. |
| **Content Burnout** | Batch content creation. Dedicate 1 day/week to creating 2 weeks of content at once. Use templates and systems. |
| **Low Initial Traction** | Patience + iteration. First releases rarely explode. Focus on consistent output, playlist adds, and algorithm signals. Aim for 10% monthly growth. |
| **Licensing Catalog Drowning in Competition** | Niche down. Don't compete on "generic upbeat background music." Find gaps — "dark cinematic trap for true crime YouTubers" or "lo-fi beats for study streamers." |
| **Overextending Across 3 Artists** | If one artist clearly outperforms the others by Week 8, consider shifting more resources to the winner. Don't force equal investment if the data says otherwise. |
